Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2025

Skool Member Tagger (the "Extension") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Extension handles and how.

Scope

This policy applies to the Skool Member Tagger Chrome extension. It does not cover the Skool platform or any other websites or services you access while using the Extension.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The Extension does not send data to our servers, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Local Processing Only

To provide its functionality (for example, identifying and tagging community members within Skool pages you open), the Extension may temporarily read and process page content in your browser. All such processing occurs locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Permissions and Purpose

The Extension requests only the minimum permissions needed to function. These permissions are used solely to provide features and not to track you or collect personal information. As listed in the extension manifest (MV3):

  • host_permissions: https://www.skool.com/* — allow interaction with Skool pages you open.
  • activeTab — enable features on the tab you explicitly activate.
  • scripting — inject code required for on-page tagging features.
  • storage — store non-personal, local preferences.
  • contextMenus — add helpful right‑click menu actions.

Content scripts: The manifest includes a content script limited to https://www.skool.com/*. It runs read‑only logic on Skool pages you open to determine whether features should activate. Any page content access is processed locally in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

You can view these permissions in your browser’s extension details. If our permissions change, your browser will prompt you to review and approve them.

Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or SDKs that collect user data through the Extension.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not share, sell, or rent any information.

Security

Because the Extension does not transmit or store personal information on our systems, risk is reduced. Still, you should keep your browser and operating system updated and use strong device security.

Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and we knowingly collect no information from children.

Your Privacy Rights

Where applicable (e.g., under GDPR/UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have certain rights regarding personal data. As the Extension does not collect personal data, these rights are generally not implicated. If you have questions, contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated “Last updated” date. Please review periodically.
